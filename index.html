Isle of Palms Beach Chair & Umbrella Rentals — Beach Bums IOP

Make your beach day effortless with Beach Bums, the local choice for comfortable chair and umbrella rentals on Isle of Palms. We provide guaranteed same-day setup, free delivery on all orders, and friendly local service you can count on. Book online for daily or weekly rentals and let us handle the heavy lifting — you just show up and relax.

What to expect renting from Beach Bums

  • Chairs & umbrellas are set up by 9:30 AM at your preferred location on Isle of Palms (selected at checkout).
  • Chairs & umbrellas are removed daily after 5:30 PM.
  • Note: We cannot set up behind the County Park or at the Grand Pavilion in Wild Dunes. We do not set up on Sullivan’s Island or Folly Beach.
  • Online reservations can be made up until 12:00 AM ET the day of your rental period. For late or same-day rentals, call/text 843-754-0102.

Frequently asked questions

Explore the FAQs regarding our rentals.

How will I find my chairs and umbrellas I rented?
At checkout you’ll be prompted for the location you would like your chairs. Chairs & umbrellas are tagged with the renter’s name. Beach Bums can set up chairs anywhere on Isle of Palms with the exception of the Grand Pavilion (in Wild Dunes) and behind the county park.

Visiting and not sure what location is closest to you? No problem — contact Beach Bums and we can help!

Text/Call: 843-754-0102
What if my plans changed and need to cancel?
Cancellations and refunds are allowed 24 hours prior to your rental period. See the Privacy Policy and contact Beach Bums for additional questions and information on cancellation policies.

Text/Call: 843-754-0102
Can I get a last minute or same day reservation?
Online reservations can be made so long as the order is received by 7:00 PM ET the day before your reservation. For last-minute or same-day reservations, contact Beach Bums directly for availability.

Text/Call: 843-754-0102
Can I create custom bundles for chairs and umbrellas?
Yes! Items can be added to your order individually or you can choose from our bundle options.

Example: Looking to book 5 chairs and 2 umbrellas? Choose 2 of our $48/day 2-chair/1-umbrella bundles and add an extra chair.

Contact us for discounts on large bundles or questions about ordering.
