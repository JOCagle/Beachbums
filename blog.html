The Best Beachfront Restaurants & Bars Near Isle of Palms

After a day in the sun, check out these top Isle of Palms restaurants and bars:

Oceanfront & Casual Dining

  • Coconut Joe’s Beach Grill – A tropical-themed spot serving seafood and frozen drinks.
  • Papi’s Taqueria – Fresh tacos and margaritas just steps from the beach.
  • Acme Lowcountry Kitchen – A favorite for Southern seafood classics and brunch.
  • The Boathouse at Breach Inlet – Stunning sunset views with fresh seafood and steak.

Bars & Nightlife

  • The Windjammer – A laid-back beach bar with live music and ocean views.
  • Smugglers Island Eats & Rum Bar – Fun cocktails and island vibes.
  • The Dinghy – A relaxed bar with local craft beer and live entertainment.
  • The Refuge – Coffee shop by day, cocktail bar by night with great small plates.

The Best Beach Access Points on Isle of Palms

If you're looking for a great spot to set up your beach chair rentals and umbrellas, here are the most popular avenue accesses on Isle of Palms:

  • Grand Pavilion Dr. Access – The most popular beach access, near shops, restaurants, and public parking.
  • Coconut Joes Access – A busy entrance with easy access to public restrooms and showers.
  • 25th Avenue Access – A great spot for families, a wide access with golf cart parking.
  • 28th Avenue Access – Popular for its easy access and nearby parking options. It is close by to the IOP Rec center.
  • 34th Avenue Access – Handicap accessible with a boardwalk that makes getting to the sand easy.
