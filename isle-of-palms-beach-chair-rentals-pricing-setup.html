How Beach Chair Rentals Work on Isle of Palms

Renting beach chairs and umbrellas on Isle of Palms is straightforward. Here's the process from start to finish:

  1. Book online or call/text. Head to our online booking flow and select your stay type, dates, beach access point, and gear. The whole process takes about two minutes. For same-day rentals, call or text 843‑754‑0102 and we'll check availability.
