Why Wild Dunes Is One of the Best Places to Stay on Isle of Palms

If you're planning a beach vacation to Isle of Palms, there's a good chance Wild Dunes Resort is already on your radar. Tucked away on the northeast end of the island behind a private gate, Wild Dunes is a 1,600-acre oceanfront resort community with everything you need for a Lowcountry getaway — championship golf, tennis and pickleball courts, resort pools, a full-service spa, on-site dining, and direct access to some of the widest, least crowded stretches of beach on IOP.

But Wild Dunes isn't just a hotel. The resort is home to hundreds of privately owned homes, condos, and villas — many of which are available as vacation rentals. Whether you're looking for an oceanfront beach house for a family reunion, a one-bedroom condo for a couples' getaway, or a resort hotel room with turnkey amenities, there's a rental option near Wild Dunes that fits. This guide breaks down the different types of beach rentals available, where to book, what to expect, and how to make the most of your stay.

Types of Beach Rentals Near Wild Dunes

The rental landscape around Wild Dunes falls into three main categories: resort-managed properties inside the gates, privately managed homes and condos within Wild Dunes, and vacation rentals elsewhere on Isle of Palms that are close enough to enjoy the area without paying resort prices.

Resort Hotel Stays — Sweetgrass Inn & Boardwalk Inn

If you want the full resort experience without managing a rental property, Wild Dunes offers two on-site hotels. The Sweetgrass Inn is the flagship — a modern, full-service hotel with oceanfront rooms, a rooftop pool, a spa, and direct beach access. The Boardwalk Inn is a more intimate option located steps from the beach and home to Coastal Provisions, the resort's standout restaurant. Both hotels include full access to resort amenities like pools, golf, tennis, and the fitness center. This is the easiest option if you don't want to deal with check-in instructions, keypad codes, or bringing your own supplies — everything is handled for you.

Condos & Villas Inside Wild Dunes

The bulk of vacation rentals at Wild Dunes are condos and villas scattered throughout the resort. Popular complexes include Ocean Club (oceanfront with resort pools), Shipwatch (near the marina), Mariners Walk (tucked between the golf course and marsh), and Summer House (close to the Boardwalk Inn and beach). Units range from cozy one-bedroom condos to spacious three- and four-bedroom villas with full kitchens, washer/dryer, and screened porches.

Condos inside Wild Dunes are ideal for families and small groups who want more space than a hotel room but still want access to resort amenities. Most units booked through the official resort program or authorized local property managers include pool access, golf privileges, and use of the tennis and pickleball courts.

Private Beach Houses Inside the Gates

For larger groups — family reunions, wedding parties, or multi-family trips — Wild Dunes has privately owned beach houses available for rent. These range from three-bedroom coastal cottages to expansive five- and six-bedroom oceanfront homes with private pools, outdoor showers, and direct beach access. They're the most expensive option, but the space, privacy, and location are hard to beat if you're splitting the cost among a group.

Vacation Rentals Outside Wild Dunes on Isle of Palms

You don't have to stay inside the Wild Dunes gates to enjoy this part of the island. The upper end of Isle of Palms — roughly from 42nd Avenue to 57th Avenue — puts you within a short drive or bike ride of the resort while often coming in at a lower nightly rate. You'll find beach houses, duplexes, and smaller condos available through Airbnb, VRBO, and local property managers. The trade-off is that you won't have access to Wild Dunes resort amenities (pools, golf, tennis) unless you purchase a day pass or your property manager has a specific arrangement. But you'll still have full access to IOP's public beaches, restaurants, and everything else the island offers.

Where to Book — And What to Watch For

There are several ways to book a beach rental near Wild Dunes, and the right one depends on what matters most to you.

Official Wild Dunes Resort

Booking directly through wilddunesresort.com is the most straightforward option. You'll get full access to resort amenities, a consistent check-in experience, and customer support from the resort itself. The resort manages a curated selection of homes, condos, and hotel rooms — including their "Reserve Collection" of luxury properties. If seamless resort access is important to you, this is the safest bet.

Local Property Management Companies

Several local companies manage large inventories of Wild Dunes properties, including Island Realty, Charleston Coast Vacations, Sweetgrass Vacation Rentals, and Carolina One Vacation Rentals. These managers often have properties that aren't listed on the official resort site and can offer more competitive pricing, especially during shoulder season. Most authorized managers also ensure their guests receive resort amenity access — but always confirm before booking.

Airbnb & VRBO

Both platforms have hundreds of listings in and around Wild Dunes. You'll find the widest range of prices and property types here, and it's a great way to compare options side by side. The key thing to verify is whether resort amenity access is included. Some privately managed listings on Airbnb and VRBO do not include access to Wild Dunes pools, golf, or fitness facilities — which can be a surprise if you assumed it was part of the deal. Always read the listing details carefully or message the host before booking.

What to Know Before You Book

  • Book early for summer. Wild Dunes is one of the most popular beach destinations near Charleston, and prime summer weeks (June–August) fill up fast — especially oceanfront properties. If you're planning a peak-season trip, start looking at least three to four months out.
  • Check the cancellation policy. Policies vary widely between the official resort, local managers, and third-party platforms. Some offer flexible cancellation; others require full payment 30–60 days in advance with no refund. Know what you're agreeing to.
  • Confirm amenity access. This is the single most important thing to verify when booking through a third-party platform. Not all Wild Dunes rentals include access to resort pools, golf courses, or the spa — and purchasing day passes separately adds up quickly.
  • Ask about parking and gate access. Wild Dunes is a gated community. Your rental should come with a gate pass or access code. Some properties have limited parking, especially condos — confirm how many vehicles are allowed before you arrive.
  • Pet policies vary. Some Wild Dunes rentals are pet-friendly, but many are not. If you're traveling with a dog, filter for pet-friendly properties and check for any breed or size restrictions.

Things to Do Near Wild Dunes

Wild Dunes has plenty to keep you busy on-property — two championship golf courses (Links and Harbor), a tennis and pickleball center, resort pools, and the Spa at Sweetgrass. But some of the best parts of an Isle of Palms vacation are off-resort.

  • Hit the beach. The stretch of sand near Wild Dunes is some of the widest and quietest on IOP. For a full breakdown of every beach access point on the island, check out our complete beach access guide.
  • Eat your way through IOP. From oceanfront seafood at Coconut Joe's to sunset cocktails at The Boathouse at Breach Inlet, the island has serious dining. Read our 2026 restaurant and bar guide for the full rundown.
  • Explore Charleston. Downtown Charleston is about 30 minutes from Wild Dunes. King Street shopping, historic tours, and world-class restaurants are all within easy reach.
  • Kayak or paddleboard the Intracoastal. The calm waters on the back side of the island are perfect for kayaking, paddleboarding, and wildlife spotting — dolphins, pelicans, and the occasional sea turtle.
  • Day trip to Sullivan's Island. Just across Breach Inlet, Sullivan's Island has its own laid-back charm — great restaurants, a historic lighthouse, and Fort Moultrie.
